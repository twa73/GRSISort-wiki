These are the best resources for information on True-coincidence summing corrections that are necessary to obtain an accurate efficiency curve for large-volume HPGe crystals.

Ryan Dunlop's (Uni. of Guelph) PhD thesis:
https://atrium.lib.uoguelph.ca/xmlui/handle/10214/16278

A guide written by Mike Bowry (Former GRIFFIN post-doc at TRIUMF and now faculty at Uni. of West Scotland):
https://gamma.triumf.ca/large-projects/GRIFFIN/documentation/GRIFFIN_Summing_Corrections_Guide_v0.0.pdf/view
